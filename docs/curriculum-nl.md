# Scenely Dutch curriculum (nl-NL)

This is the plan for which scenes Scenely teaches, in which order, from no Dutch at all to B2.
It follows the same climb as the usual NT2 course series (level 0 → A2, A2 → B1, B1 → B2) and the
parts of the inburgering exam, but **every dialogue in Scenely is written for Scenely**. Nothing is
copied from textbooks or from DUO practice exams: those are copyrighted. Learners can use their own
course books and the free DUO practice material next to the app.

Sources for this plan are public: the CEFR level descriptions (Council of Europe) and the exam
information on inburgeren.nl (exam parts, levels and the KNM learning goals). Check inburgeren.nl
for the current rules before relying on the exam notes below; they change from time to time.

## How a level is built

Each level has:

- **Can-do goals**: what the learner can do at the end, in plain words.
- **Situations**: the places on the map and the scenes (one per role) that practise them.
- **Words and grammar**: what the dialogues may use. Scenes stay inside their level's limits, so a
  learner is never surprised by grammar they have not met.
- **Exam link**: which part of the inburgering exam the scenes prepare for.

Every scene also keeps the Scenely rules: Dutch only, no translations, meaning from pictures and
choices, and a speaking exercise after each right answer.

## Level 0 → A1: first contact

**Can-do**
- Greet, say goodbye, say thank you and please (*alstublieft, dank u wel*).
- Ask for one thing and say how many (*een koffie, twee broodjes*).
- Understand prices and pay (*pinnen of contant*).
- Say your name, where you come from and where you live.
- Understand simple questions with *wat, waar, hoeveel, wilt u*.

**Words and grammar**
- Present tense of regular verbs and *zijn, hebben, willen, kunnen*.
- *u* and *je*; *de* and *het* for common nouns; numbers 1–100, prices in euros, times on the hour.
- Short main clauses only, verb second (*Ik wil een koffie. Wilt u een tas?*).
- Yes/no questions with the verb first.

**Situations on the map**

| Place | Scenes | Practises |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Bij de koffiebar | Klant ✓, Barista ✓ | ordering, *met melk of zwart*, paying |
| Supermarkt | Klant ✓, Kassa ✓ | greeting, a bag, the total, paying, the receipt |
| Op de markt | Klant ✓, Kaasboer ✓ | weight and amounts (*een ons, een kilo*), tasting, *nog iets?* |

**Exam link**: the start of *Spreken* and *Luisteren* (short exchanges in shops).

## A1 → A2: everyday life on your own

**Can-do**
- Ask where something is and understand simple directions.
- Make and change an appointment (day, time, *volgende week*).
- Describe a simple problem: you feel ill, something is broken, you are lost.
- Talk about your day and what you did yesterday.
- Fill in simple forms: name, address, date of birth.

**Words and grammar**
- Perfect tense (*ik heb gekocht, ik ben gegaan*); separable verbs (*opbellen, meenemen*).
- Modal verbs with an infinitive at the end (*ik wil een afspraak maken*).
- Dates, clock times, days and months; *er* in simple uses (*er is, er zijn*).
- Linking with *en, maar, want, of*.

**Situations on the map**

| Place | Scenes | Practises |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Kledingwinkel | Klant ✓, Verkoper ✓ | sizes and colours, the fitting room, exchanging something |
| Huisarts | Patiënt ✓, Huisarts ✓ | saying what hurts and since when, coming back next week |
| Station | Reiziger ✓, Loket ✓ | a ticket, platform and times, a delay |
| *Bibliotheek* (new) | Bezoeker, Medewerker | a library card, borrowing, the opening hours |
| *Apotheek* (new) | Klant, Apotheker | picking up medicine, how often to take it |

**Exam link**: *Luisteren* and *Lezen* (announcements, short notes, opening hours);
KNM themes *gezondheid en zorg*, *instanties*.

## A2 → B1: dealing with work, home and the town hall

**Can-do**
- Explain a situation and give reasons (*omdat, want*), say what you think about it.
- Handle a phone call with an organisation and follow up by e-mail.
- Talk with a teacher, a landlord, a colleague, a neighbour.
- Understand a letter from the gemeente and do what it asks.
- Tell a story about something that happened, in order.

**Words and grammar**
- Subordinate clauses with the verb at the end (*omdat ik ziek ben, als het regent*).
- Past simple (*ik werkte, ik ging*) next to the perfect tense.
- Comparatives and superlatives; polite requests (*zou u ... kunnen*).
- Formal and informal register: when to say *u* and when *je*.

**Situations on the map**

| Place | Scenes | Practises |
| --- | --- | --- |
| School | Ouder ✓, Leraar ✓ | a talk about your child, the school's rules, an absence |
| Ziekenhuis | Patiënt ✓, Verpleegkundige ✓ | registering, finding the department, after-care |
| *Gemeente* (new) | Inwoner, Medewerker | registering an address, a letter, an appointment at the desk |
| *Woning* (new) | Huurder, Verhuurder | a repair, the rent, rules in the building |
| *Werk* (new) | Sollicitant, Werkgever | a job interview, your experience, working hours |
| *Buren* (new) | Nieuwe buur, Buurvrouw | introducing yourself, noise, a birthday visit |

**Exam link**: the B1 parts of the inburgering exam (*Lezen, Luisteren, Schrijven, Spreken*);
KNM themes *werk en inkomen*, *wonen*, *onderwijs en opvoeding*, *instanties*,
*omgangsvormen, waarden en normen*; ONA (orientation on the Dutch labour market).

## B1 → B2: confident and precise

**Can-do**
- Take part in a discussion, give arguments and respond to someone else's.
- Explain a complex problem and suggest solutions.
- Understand the news, a radio item, a work meeting.
- Write a formal complaint or a motivation letter.

**Words and grammar**
- Passive voice; relative clauses; indirect speech.
- Fixed expressions and collocations; nuance (*eigenlijk, toch, wel*).
- Formal letters and e-mails; register changes within one conversation.

**Situations on the map**: longer, branching scenes at places learners already know, for example
a complaint at the Kledingwinkel, a work meeting at Werk, a council meeting at the Gemeente.

**Exam link**: Staatsexamen NT2 (programma II is B2).

## KNM: knowledge of Dutch society

KNM scenes are short situations with a choice of what to do, written for Scenely around the
exam's public themes, for example:

- **Werk en inkomen**: a payslip, sick leave, the UWV.
- **Gezondheid en zorg**: the huisarts first, the pharmacy, health insurance.
- **Wonen**: rent, the landlord, waste and recycling, neighbours.
- **Onderwijs en opvoeding**: school age, the parents' evening, childcare.
- **Instanties**: the gemeente, DUO, the Belastingdienst.
- **Geschiedenis en staatsinrichting**: elections, King's Day, Liberation Day.
- **Omgangsvormen, waarden en normen**: appointments and being on time, greeting, equal rights.

These fit best as a separate scene type (a situation with "what do you do?" choices) shown at the
place where it happens: a KNM question about health insurance at the Huisarts, for example.

## What the map needs for this plan

- The eight places drawn today cover Level 0 → A2 and part of A2 → B1.
- New places to draw for A2 → B1: Bibliotheek, Apotheek, Gemeente, Woning, Werk, Buren.
- Each place keeps two roles, so learners hear both sides of every conversation.

## Writing rules for scene authors

1. Write new Dutch dialogue; never copy or closely paraphrase a course book or exam.
2. Stay inside the level's grammar; one new structure per scene at most.
3. Every wrong answer must be clearly wrong in the situation, never a trick.
4. Keep sentences speakable: they are also the speaking exercise.
5. Use typographic quotes only, and euro amounts as *€7,40*.
